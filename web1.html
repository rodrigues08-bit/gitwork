Our Story

Cake Canvas began as a passion project in a tiny sunlit kitchen, where frosting was our paint and cakes were our canvas. Founded by a girl who believed emotions taste better when baked, we mix creativity with every spoon of batter.

What started as weekend experiments turned into a brand that tells stories in every slice. From heartbreaks to birthdays, rainy evenings to sunny smiles—there’s a cake for every feeling!

We believe cake isn’t just dessert — it’s comfort, celebration, healing, and art. At Cake Canvas, we don’t just bake … we express.
